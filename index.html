About

How a finance background turned into a career reading the story behind the numbers.

My journey into data analytics began on the finance side of the business — reconciliations, forecasting models, project dashboards. Over time, I realised what excited me most wasn't closing the books, it was uncovering the patterns underneath them, asking why, and turning raw numbers into decisions that mattered.

Today I combine that finance foundation, stakeholder management, and project ownership with hands-on technical skills in SQL, Python, R, and data visualisation. I understand both the business context and the people behind the data, and how decisions ripple through operations at scale. Above all, I believe data is most powerful when it leads to action.
